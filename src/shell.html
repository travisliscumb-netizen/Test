OPERATION
BLACKGATE

A chemical weapons facility cut into the rock. Get to the control room, pull the research data off the terminal, and be out through the plant before the garrison works out you were ever inside.

Runs entirely offline. Audio starts on your first tap — that is a browser rule, not a bug.

Eyes only

Briefing

Situation

Blackgate processes and stores nerve agent under a research cover. Its garrison is small but disciplined, and the plant floor has enough pressurised stock to make a firefight a poor idea.

Objective A

Reach the control room and download the research archive from the terminal.

Objective B

Exit through the chemical plant. The exit stays sealed until the archive is yours.

Opposition

Guards patrol the corridors and will call the alarm on sight. Lab staff are unarmed — they run, and they raise the alarm too. Shots are loud; the suppressed PP7 is not.

Field manual

Controls

Touch

Left half of the screen is the movement stick, right half is the look stick — both appear wherever your thumb lands. Push the movement stick to its rim to sprint. FIRE and WEAP sit under your right thumb, CRCH crouches, and USE appears in amber when something can be operated.

Keyboard & mouse

WASD move · mouse look · left click fire · Q/E cycle weapons · 1-3 select · C crouch · Shift sprint · E interact · R reload · Esc pause. Click the view to lock the mouse where the browser allows it; dragging works everywhere.

Reading the facility

Fluorescents are cool white while you are undetected and switch to pulsing red the moment the alarm goes. Guards look at their patrol path; scientists do not fight.
