Drag anywhere in the left pad to move. Holding away from your opponent blocks. Punch confirms in menus and Kick goes back. Super lights up after you hit the falling power orb.

Fighting

Block
Hold away. Crouch to block sweeps, stand for jump-ins.
Specials
Down, down-forward, forward + button (more in the pause menu)
Throw
Forward + punch, up close
Super
Hit the falling power orb, then press punch + kick together
