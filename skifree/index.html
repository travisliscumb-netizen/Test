Controls

← → / A DSteer. Keep pushing across the hill to stop, then sidestep.
↑ / WPoint straight downhill and tuck
↓ / SSnowplough brake
SpaceHop. Ramps launch you properly.
In the air↑ backflip · ↓ spread eagle · Space helicopter. Land it or eat snow.
FGo fast. Very fast. Harder to steer.
Esc / P / F3Pause
R / F2Restart run
MouseThe skier heads toward the pointer. Click to hop.
TouchTouch and hold anywhere: the skier heads for your finger. Flick up or down for tricks. HOP and TURBO on the right (arrow buttons in Options if you prefer).

The three courses at the top are timed: ski through the START arch, thread the gates (each miss costs 5 s) and cross FINISH. Big air and landed tricks earn style. And keep moving: the Abominable Snow Monster doesn't like dawdlers.

About

SkiFree — a from-scratch modern tribute to the 1991 Windows classic by Chris Pirih.

Every line of code, every drawing and every sound here is new: the art is vector-drawn at runtime and the audio is synthesised live. Nothing is taken from the original.
