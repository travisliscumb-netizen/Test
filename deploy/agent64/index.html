Mission 01 · Black Current

Situation

The Kelvara hydroelectric research station sits under a category-three storm, and its night shift is thin. Beneath the turbine floor, the station's sponsors are holding a classified data core we were told did not exist.

Primary

Infiltrate the control wing and extract the data core from the vault terminal.

Secondary

Disable the three surveillance relays. Do it before the alarm sounds and the record stays clean.

Egress

The service dock stays open for ninety seconds after the core leaves its cradle. Reach the extraction pad.

Two routes exist. The maintenance culvert on the west wall is quiet and cramped. The main gate is fast and loud. Both reach the vault.

Controls

Touch

Left thumb drags the movement stick — push it to the rim to sprint. Right half of the screen drags to look. FIRE, AIM, RLD (reload), CRCH (crouch) and SWAP sit under the right thumb. USE appears in amber when something can be interacted with. The pause key is top-left.

Keyboard & mouse

WASD move · mouse look · left button fire · right button aim · Shift sprint · C crouch · R reload · 1/2/3 weapon select · E interact · Esc pause. Click the view once to capture the mouse where the browser allows it; dragging works everywhere.

Reading the world

Cyan is friendly or interactive. Amber marks objectives. Red means an alarm or damage. A guard's shoulder light tells you its state: cyan patrol, amber suspicious, red hunting.
