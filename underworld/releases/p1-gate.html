How the new road network works

Kept straightEdge streets between districts, the arterials and the waterfront stay straight. They are the old lattice's backbone, and lane traffic, signals and the races still use them.
High streetsEach district has one or two winding main streets with shops, benches and kerb parking (where the street is at least 11 m wide), for example Saint Ivo Street, Linden Parade, Central Blvd, Meridian Way, Northgate Road and Neon Row.
Lanes and closesSmaller streets fork off the high streets and join edge streets at least 14 m from any crossing. Cul-de-sacs end in turning circles, and houses face them.
RoundaboutsExchange Circus (financial core) and Meridian Circus (Uptown), where several streets meet at a district's heart, plus the six island roundabouts. Traffic gives way on entry and goes round anticlockwise.
EntrancesWhere an island road reaches a district, the district's street continues it as a crossroads: Lakeside Avenue into Meridian Way, the Coast Highway into Crestmoor Drive and Parkside Crescent, Sound Parkway into Anchor Street.
LandmarksLandmark cells stand where they always stood. The new streets run past their entrances: Meridian Motors' drive, the Royale's door, the gas stations' forecourts.

Generation got slower first, then I fixed it. Partway through the gate the city took 40 s to build, double the old 20.7 s, because the district kit tested every candidate lot against every point of every street. Each lot now checks only the streets near it, using the same distance test. Boot is down to 22.0 s and the generated world is byte-for-byte identical.

Still on the grid, and why

  • The port's north end (10,0, 11,0, 11,1) and 11,4. These sit beside the two paved yards. Rebuilding them would close a street the yards' rules need (see decision 2).
  • Harborview Mall and the cell south of it (6,8, 6,9). The mall's car park opens onto the street between them.
  • The Velvet bonded store and the tow yard (6,10, 6,11). The street between the two warehouse yards carries their back doors, and nothing narrower than it fits there.
