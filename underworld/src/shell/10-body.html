Touch

Left side: drag anywhere for the movement stick (steering while driving). Right side: drag to look around. ATK: tap to punch, hold for a heavy blow. BLK: hold to block, tap while moving to dodge. PHONE opens your empire. A gamepad also works (left stick, right stick, triggers to drive, A jump, B/Y enter, X attack).

Touch: guns

With a gun out the right side becomes a shooter layout. Left thumb moves, right thumb aims (drag anywhere on the right), index finger holds FIRE — all three at once. You can also drag on FIRE itself to aim while shooting. ADS toggles precision aim (tighter spread, slower look). RLD reloads, HIT appears when someone is within reach. Aim assist (Settings → Controls) slows the reticle over a target and nudges it toward the torso; it never turns you toward anyone behind you.
