Tips

  • Keep one column open and save the I-piece for a Tetris.
  • Rotate a T into a slot under an overhang for a T-Spin. The piece must end with a rotation, not a move.
  • Chain Tetrises and T-Spins with no ordinary clears in between for back-to-back bonuses.
  • A piece on the floor locks after 1 second. You can move or rotate to reset that timer up to 30 times.
