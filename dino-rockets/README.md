# Grayson's Spellosaurus

A spelling game for a six-year-old. It's a single HTML file that works offline.
The week's spelling words become an adventure across Dino Island, with one
stop per word. He picks a crew of four dinosaurs, who clear the letters at
each stop:
- Every right answer warms up an egg.
- Every finished stop hatches that egg into a baby dino with its own super
  power, and he can put it in his crew.
- The week ends with a boss battle against the Grumpy Volcano. Calming it
  down wins a golden egg, and fossils for hats and gear.

Open `graysons-dino-rockets.html` in Safari or Chrome. The file name hasn't
changed, so an existing home-screen icon or bookmark keeps working, and so
does all saved progress. On an iPad or iPhone, use **Share → Add to Home
Screen** and it runs full-screen with its own icon. Nothing is sent anywhere:
words and progress are saved on the device.

## For grown-ups

- Tap the gear on the home screen. The passcode is **9999**.
- Type or paste the week's list. Commas, spaces, numbering and new lines all
  work. Words need 2 to 12 letters, and a week can have up to 30.
- Any change to the list starts a new adventure. Stars earned on a word are
  kept, so a word that comes back later starts from where he left it.
- **How it's going** shows 0–3 stars per word. A star is earned for each
  clean spell from memory. A word that needed help loses one star and gets
  its practice steps back.
- If a phone says "A" oddly when spelling, choose another way of saying A
  or E under **Letter voice** and tap Test.

## The crew

Six dinosaurs to start with, and every baby he hatches.

| Dino | Species | Moves |
|---|---|---|
| Rex | T. rex | Big Roar, Tiny Arms (tail whip), Chomp Chase, Jet Jump |
| Trike | Triceratops | Stomp & Charge, Stomp & Stack, Bounce House, Tow Truck, Boost Jump (team-up) |
| Dash | Raptor | Speed Sweep, Whirlwind, Lasso Round-up, High Jump |
| Swoop | Pterosaur | Dive Bomb, Joy Ride, Sky Hook |
| Stretch | Brachiosaurus | Crane Neck (stretches up to the letters), Neck Slide (they slide down his neck onto his back), Neck Lift (team-up: he lifts a buddy up to the letters) |
| Spike | Stegosaurus | Letter Rain (letters drop onto his back plates as he walks under them), Tail Flip (his tail flips them up onto his plates) |

- **Anyone who walks:** Tightrope. **Anyone:** Geyser Surprise, Toss Relay,
  Tug of War and the Dino Parade.
- **Super powers:** each baby also has one of its own: Lasso, Bubble Blower,
  Super Magnet, Tunnel Digger, Frost Breath or Vine Swinger.
- **Stretch and Spike** are new. The home screen introduces them until he
  has been to Dino Base to meet them, and he can swap them into the crew
  there.
- **Rescues:** in some scenes a letter falls. For example, Trike stomps, stacks the
  letters on his horns, and the top one wobbles off as he walks away. A buddy
  comes to the rescue in his own way and often hands it back:
  - Dash lassos it.
  - A magnet baby pulls it in.
  - Swoop lowers a hook.
  - Stretch reaches over with his long neck.
  - Spike flips it with his tail.
  - Rex jets in and snaps it up.
  - Trike charges and scoops it on his horns.
  - Tunnel, frost and bubble babies each use their power.
- **Dino Base:** everyone he has hatched lives here. Tap a dino, then a crew
  spot, to swap it in. The crew of four is who performs in the scenes and
  hangs out on the island.
- **Levels:** every scene earns experience (the lead earns most), and so does
  every finished stop.
  - Everyone starts at level 1. Grown-ups can put the crew back to level 1
    from the grown-ups screen.
  - Level 2 brings rope tricks.
  - Level 3 brings Tightrope and the team-ups.
  - Level 4 earns a gold star badge, and level 5 a super star.
  - Babies grow up as they level: baby, then kid, then grown.
- **Fossils and Dress Up:** every finished stop digs up a fossil, and calming
  the volcano digs up three more. Fossils are never spent. The more he has,
  the more hats there are in **Dress Up** for any dino:
  - party hat (free)
  - cowboy hat (3)
  - cool shades (6)
  - propeller cap (10)
  - pirate hat (15)
  - chef hat (20)
  - knight helmet (26)
  - flower crown (32)
  - royal crown (40)
- **Upgrading from Dino Rockets:**
  - All progress, babies and crew are kept.
  - Babies hatched before keep their exact looks and powers.
  - Each rocket part already won counts as a boss win: 3 fossils.

## How a word is practised

Each stop runs a short set of activities. Which ones depends on how well he
already knows the word:

| Stars | Activities |
|---|---|
| 0 (new) | Meet → Munch → Build → Spell it |
| 1 | Meet → Letter Race → Missing pieces → Spell it |
| 2 | Meet → Spell Check → Rhyme Time (or Missing pieces) → Spell it |
| 3 | Meet → Letter Race or Spell Check (taking turns) → Rhyme Time (or Missing pieces) → Spell it |

- **Meet:** the word is shown and spelled aloud. Tap any letter to hear it.
- **Munch:** jungle leaves carrying look-alike words drift past, and he
  munches the word he hears (2 rounds).
- **Build:** he taps or drags the letters into the egg's warm-up line, in
  order.
- **Missing pieces:** he fills the gaps from 3 believable letters. The gaps
  go where he has slipped before.
- **Letter Race:** the word flashes up and disappears, then he races a
  long-neck to catch its letters as they float around. Winning the race is a
  bonus; finishing always counts.
- **Spell Check:** he picks the right spelling from three. The wrong ones
  are believable mistakes, never real words. If a grown-up has added an
  example sentence for the word (the ✎ button in the word list), it is shown
  with a blank.
- **Rhyme Time:** only for words in a hand-checked list of true rhyme
  families, so no eye-rhymes like COME/HOME. The shared ending is
  highlighted.
- **Spell it:** he spells the word from memory. Two misses on the same letter
  flash the word and light the key. That counts as help, so the word keeps
  its practice next time.

**The egg:**
- Every activity except Meet has a nest with the egg that will hatch at
  this stop, in its colours.
- Each right letter or answer sends a warm spark into the warm-up line, and
  the egg glows brighter and rocks harder.
- When it's full it's HOT and shaking.
- After "Spell it" comes **3, 2, 1, ROAR!**: the crew roars, the island
  shakes and the egg jumps.
- Then comes a crew scene, and then he taps the egg to hatch it.

**Scenes:**
- There are 26 scenes, plus the rescues. Scenes run about 10 seconds (up to
  about 22), and no one grabs a letter or leaves the screen in a blur.
  Leads usually show off as they arrive and do a victory move before they
  leave.
- Some scenes bring a buddy who watches, gasps and cheers.
- The lead is whoever has waited longest. A scene never comes back within 3
  plays of itself, and if one has to repeat within 10, it plays the other way
  round.

**The Grumpy Volcano:**
- When every stop is done, the volcano brings back up to 4 of the shakiest
  words to spell from memory once more (a spaced review).
- Each one sends a crew roar at it, and it gets a little less grumpy.
- Once it's calm it smiles and pops out a golden egg.
- Next comes the **Victory Show**, which runs about 40 seconds: each crew
  member performs one of the week's words, then the crew takes a bow.
  Grown-ups can skip it.
- Last, he cracks open the golden egg for a golden baby.

The design follows the research on early spelling:
- **Retrieval, not copying:** the word is hidden for "Spell it".
- **Orthographic mapping:** letter names are heard as each letter is placed.
- **Spacing:** the volcano review.
- **Mastery-based fading of support:** the activities get harder as a word
  earns stars.

If he stalls for 9 seconds, he gets a nudge: the prompt again, and a buddy
waving at the answer.

## Developing

The sources live in `src/` and are joined into the one shipped file by
`tools/assemble.mjs`.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `00-shell.html` | screens |
| `10-ui.css` | interface (phone, small phone and tablet sizes) |
| `15-stage.css` | the crew layer |
| `20-core.js` | pure logic (tested in node): words, mastery, the island, the crew, rescues, fossils, the scene planner and validator |
| `30-speech.js` | speech controller |
| `40-sfx.js` | synthesised sound effects and music |
| `50-dinos.js` | character art and hats |
| `60-stage.js` | scene executor (with Stretch's neck rig) and the ambient crew |
| `80-activities.js` | the activities and the egg nest |
| `90-app.js` | the adventure flow |

```
npm run build          # rebuild graysons-dino-rockets.html
npm test               # core + speech unit tests, and a check the built file is current
npm run test:app       # end-to-end playtest in a real browser, with screenshots at 3 sizes
npm run qa:scenes      # every crew scene, normal and mirrored, with filmstrips
node tools/scene-qa.mjs --rescues   # Stomp & Stack once with every kind of rescue
npm run qa:glitch      # every scene with every possible lead, traced frame by frame for snaps and pops
node tools/make-icon.mjs && npm run build   # after changing the art
```

The browser tests use Playwright with the Chromium at `/opt/pw-browsers`.
Screenshots go to `../.shots/dino-app` and filmstrips to
`../.shots/dino-scenes`.

URL switches for testing:
- `?mute`: no sound.
- `?speed=4`: crew scenes at 4× speed.
- `?fast`: short treks between stops.
