    HOW TO PLAY

    Clear every dot to finish the maze. Touching a ghost costs a life.

    A power pellet turns the ghosts blue for a few seconds. Eat them in a row for 200, 400, 800, 1600. Bonus fruit appears twice a level.

    Each ghost hunts differently: Blinky chases you directly, Pinky cuts ahead, Inky flanks with Blinky, and Clyde loses his nerve up close.

    Extra life at 10,000. Ghosts slow down in the side tunnels. Turning early around a corner is faster than turning late.
