    How to play

    Clear every pellet to finish the maze. Touching a ghost costs a life.

    A power pellet turns the ghosts blue. Chain them for 200 · 400 · 800 · 1600.

    Blinky chases, Pinky cuts you off, Inky flanks, and Clyde loses his nerve up close.

    Extra life every 10,000 on Easy (once, on Arcade). A new maze every three levels. Ghosts slow in the side tunnels, and turning early around a corner is faster.
